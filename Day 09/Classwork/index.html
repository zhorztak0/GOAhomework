Pizza

Essential pizza ingredients include dough (flour, yeast, water, salt, olive oil), tomato sauce, and mozzarella cheese. Popular toppings include pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, bell peppers, sausage, and basil. For best results, use bread flour for the crust, thick sauce (passata or crushed tomatoes), and fresh or shredded mozzarella.
