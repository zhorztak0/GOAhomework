About UFC

The Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) is the world's largest mixed martial arts (MMA) promotion company. Founded in 1993, it hosts global events featuring fighters competing in an eight-sided steel cage called the Octagon. Athletes use multiple disciplines like boxing, wrestling, and jiu-jitsu under unified rules.
